How it works

  1. 01

    Report what you know.

    Add a photo, the date and an approximate area. Keep one identifying detail private so you can check that the right person has the item.

    A clear photo can show the item’s shape and colour without exposing private details.

  2. 02

    Search what you remember.

    Look through reports by category, date and area. A possible match is a starting point, not proof of ownership.

    Similar items can look alike. Check a detail that was not in the public report.

  3. 03

    Arrange a safe return.

    Start with a message request. Agree the details together, meet in a public place, then mark the return when it happens.

    Choose a public meeting place and agree on the time before you set out.

A safer return starts with care.

  • Meet in a public place.
  • Never send money to prove ownership.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Keep identifying details private. Your exact location is yours to share.

Norfold is in beta. A match, a response or a return is not guaranteed.

A place to begin looking.

Norfold is a lost-and-found network for reporting, matching and recovering items. Reports are visible to signed-in readers, and Norfold does not decide who owns an item or handle payment between people.
