—
  • Readable floor 200px, structural floor 182px (four 44px action targets and 2px gaps). Below 200px the author name is gone and the place label wraps; below 182px the action row itself overflows.
  • Arithmetic is the app's: container = canvas − 2 × 16px × text%, column = (container − (n−1) × 12) / n.
  • Needs — canvas for this count at the app's 260px card floor (boundary = 272n + 28, from the app's 40px inset budget).
  • Product canvas cap is 1152px, so 1280 and 1440 render identically here — that is the real behaviour, not a lab bug.
  • Text 200% is a stress view: the app's own maximum is 125%.
  • The preview scrolls with the wheel, trackpad or arrow keys; its scrollbar is hidden so the frame width is exactly the number you picked.
  • Titles clamped and place wraps are read from the rendered DOM in this browser and font — expect a character or two of drift on another device.
